Approver attribution (derived at render time)

sports.operation attaches :approved-by to the commit record's :payload only. sports.store/commit-record! reads :payload for :program/set and :background-check/set, but :participant/mark-certified reads neither — it re-drafts the record through sports.registry/register-certification-finalization, whose inputs are only participant-id, jurisdiction and sequence. The approver of the one real-world actuation is therefore dropped from the SSoT, and :approval-granted never reaches store/ledger either.
